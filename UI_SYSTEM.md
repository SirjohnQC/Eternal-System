- alive
- reactive
- mysterious
- powerful

---

- diegetic (feels part of the world)

---

## MAIN VIEWS

## VOXEL VISUALIZATION
- **Cross-Section View**: Allow the player to "slice" the planet to see the rocky bottom and the hidden caves beneath the environment.
- **Blocky Aesthetic**: Use high-fidelity pixel-textured voxels to maintain a "Digital God" feel.
- **Atmospheric Depth**: Clouds are semi-transparent voxel clusters, making the "Top" feel airy and distinct from the "Rocky Bottom."
- **Voxel based buiilding**: Buildings are created procedurally using voxel block
