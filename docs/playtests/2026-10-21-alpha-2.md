# Alpha playtest, round 2: 2026-10-21

Build: `main` @ `51149a8` (v0.1.0, PRs #174–#177 merged). I played in headless Chromium at 1440×900 with SwiftShader WebGL.
Screenshots are in `/tmp/claude-0/-home-claude/d23aa3ae-9b61-5aff-aa71-d66f5decf8b3/scratchpad/alpha2/`. Below I cite them by file name (e.g. `37-voice-open.png`).

**Caveat on frame rate.** The page ran at 1–3 fps for most of the fresh game, and 10–40 fps in the dev run once the cinematic was skipped. Wall-clock numbers are only useful for comparing one part of the game with another.

Runs:

1. **Fresh game** from the title screen. Home world "Calyra". The dice came up LIFE 2, EVOLUTION 18, HOSTILITY 19, ENTROPY 8, Dominion "05 DISTANT". I named the species **Thessari** and the religion **The Stilt Choir**. I played from the cinematic, through the Forge and life, to a **Transcendent Civilisation** (7.7M yrs). I used no console hooks in this run.
2. **Dev run**: `?dev=1&planet=Orrin&cine=0&forge=0&formed=1`. I played from microbes to Hands, bought Bigger Brain to pass the mind gate, and reached the first civilisation.
   - **Hooks used:** `__engine.leapToEra(3)` to reach nations quickly.
   - **Hooks used:** I set `__gameState.screen = 'game'` before Ctrl+S, because saving does nothing otherwise (Bug B1). That let me test whether the nations survive a reload.

Console: one 404 (the known mp3). There were two `[IsoDiorama] frame error TypeError: Failed to execute 'createImageData' on 'CanvasRenderingContext2D': Value is not of type 'long'.` errors, one per run, from `FloraLayer.makeSheet` (`FloraLayer.ts:392`). There were also many Canvas2D `willReadFrequently` warnings. There were no uncaught page errors.

---

## Verdict

Yes, I would keep playing, and for longer than last time.

The interruptions from round 1 are gone or tamed:

- Leaders are a toast plus a docked panel instead of a modal.
- The Overview panel is live.
- The GOD chat opens over the planet.
- Esc behaves in most places.
- The Chronicle's "What came of it" now fills in. My People panel even says "Hidden Lode: Rich veins of ore were found beneath Council of Dregaan. They still tell of it." That is the "I caused this" loop working.
- The mind gate plus the Intellect meter is the best new thing in the build. Buying Bigger Brain and watching the meter jump to "INTELLECT 5 / 10 · The mind is awake: they can found a civilisation." finally made my evolution choices matter to civilisation.

But three things still pull me out:

1. **Saving does not work at all in a normal game.** Ctrl+S and the autosave never write anything (B1). A player who closes the tab loses everything.
2. **The top centre of the screen is a pile-up.** Great-moment banners, cosmic warnings, voice toasts and tech-card toasts all stack in the same place. The moment I am supposed to savour ("Council of Dregaan leads the Thessari into the Industrial age.") is covered within a second (`m3-13-INDUSTRIAL_CIVILISATION.png`, `m3-14-EARLY_CIVILISATION.png`).
3. **The new blocking popup is "SPECIES DIVERGES".** It appears every ~100–200K years in the life stages, drops the speed to ▶ each time, and Esc does not close it.

My species still changes its name four times and is never called what I named it in the Species or Nations screens. After civilisation the game still runs from Ancient to Transcendent in about 8 minutes at 1000× with nothing to decide. 274 DNA and 60 TP sat unspent.

## The first ten minutes

1. **Title** (`01-title.png`). The screen is new: the menu is dimmed behind "TOUCH THE GLASS — something on the other side is waiting to wake". It is atmospheric and also unlocks audio. I liked it.
2. **Seed console** (`03-begin.png`, `04-rolled.png`). It now reads "ETERNAL SYSTEMS". The EVOLUTION die now says "Evolution's temper · a little quicker or a little slower", which is honest.
   - **LAUNCH SIMULATION is fully visible** after the last roll (bottom edge at y=824).
   - The Dominion roll "05 DISTANT" is still unexplained. There is no tooltip and no `title`.
3. **Big Bang cinematic** (`05-cine-*.png`, `06-cine-end.png`). It is still lovely ("Where the threads knot, galaxies are born.").
   - It took **roughly 8–9 minutes** of wall time at 1–2 fps, the same as round 1. SKIP exists, but the timing is clearly frame-based.
   - The end handoff showed the system view with "DESCEND TO CALYRA" in the DOM, but the overlay was barely visible in my capture (`06-cine-end.png`).
4. **The Forge** (`08-forge.png`, `09-forge-mid.png`). It is still the best loop in the game. It opened with "The dice were cruel. The world begins harsh.", which is a nice nod to my LIFE 2.
   - The flavour lines answered my picks: "The world grows heavier.", "Clouds gather over the cooling rock.", "The deep fires settle.", "The fumes turn sharp."

   - The panel has a large empty area. Life potential and LET IT COOL only appear after a delay.
6. **"THE LAST CRUST COOLS"** (`11-cool.png`, `12-cool-1000.png`).
   - At ▶ the countdown went 1:26 → 1:16 in 10 s.
   - At 1000× it ended within 10 s, and "A WHISPER" was already up. **Fixed.**

   - The Spark now says "Life wakes in warm tidal pools, under a pull of tides." It fits the ocean world. **Fixed.**

8. **Life awakens** (`14-life.png`). I named it Thessari. The god chat said ""Thessari" — so your children shall be known across the cosmos." The feed said "The forging is over. Calyra is **a ocean** world now".
9. **About 5 seconds later**, the first "SPECIES DIVERGES" modal appeared (`17-microbial.png`). The text was "Micro Sulphur Coral has diverged — a new lineage emerges on Calyra."
   - I had just named my species Thessari, so I did not know who "Micro Sulphur Coral" was. It turned out to be **my** species, under its biological name.
   - I chose "Intelligent" and blessed it. Within a minute a second one appeared ("Polyp has diverged…").
   - **Esc did not close it.** Instead Esc closed the Species window *under* it (`22-after-esc.png`).
10. **Species → Evolution** (`23-evo-tab.png`, `26-evolved.png`). The new INTELLECT meter is right there: "INTELLECT 0 / 10 — The mind wakes at 4. It grows slowly on its own; ✦ changes bring it sooner."
    - I queued Chloroplasts, Oxygen Breathing and Colony Bonds and evolved: "Congratulations! Polyp evolved into Lesser Sunlit Anemone! (Form II)".
    - So within five minutes my species had been called **Thessari, Micro Sulphur Coral, Polyp, Lesser Sunlit Anemone**, and later **Coral**.
11. **The first great moment** ("A GREAT MOMENT · LIFE — Cells hold together — On Calyra, single cells have begun to live as one. The world slows to ▶ so you can watch.") appeared while the Species window and two cosmic toasts were on screen. It was half-covered by "COSMIC WARNING — A void storm is approaching" (`23-evo-tab.png`). I noticed it only because I was reading the DOM.
12. **Multicellular → Complex → Primitive → Early Civilisation** took 740K → 2.5M yrs. That was about 3.5 minutes of wall time with my 1000× re-presses (`m1-*.png`).
    - Every stage change dropped the speed to ▶, which is good.
    - There were also **six "SPECIES DIVERGES" modals** in that span. Each dropped the speed to ▶ and waited for a click.
    - Intellect drifted up on its own: "The Lesser Sunlit Anemone grow cleverer (intellect 3 of 4)", then "…Their minds are awake now (intellect 4)." It took about 400K yrs. I never had to buy a mind card in this run.
    - "FIRST MINDS — The Thessari know that they are." fired once, as promised.

  - That felt great, but 0 → 5 for one card makes the "drifts slowly" path irrelevant.
  - "Needs Backbone or Exoskeleton first." is a clear lock message.

- **"Builds coral houses on stilts"** stays true at 10 billion people in the Post-Human age, though the disc shows red spires late.
- **Dominion "DISTANT"** is still unexplained.

## Missing

- **A working save** (B1), and a visible Save / Load in Settings. A brand-new player has no way to know Ctrl+S exists.
- **One lane for notifications.** Moments should own the top centre. Toasts should go to a side stack that never covers the moment, and cosmic warnings for *other* systems ("A massive asteroid is inbound toward the Nyxal system") could go to the feed only.
- **Speciation as a feed item or toast** ("✿ A new lineage split from the Thessari: NAME IT"), like the leader voices.
- **A reason to spend DNA and TP after civilisation.** I ended with 274 DNA and 60 TP (29 cards, 26 "ready") and played one TP card all game. Nothing made me want the others.
- **A setback message.** When the era fell from Industrial to Ancient (B5), I wanted a moment ("The Thessari lose their machines…") and a Chronicle entry.
- **A council that changes**: deaths, coups, new faces as ages pass.

## Top 5 recommendations (by impact on feel)

1. **Make saving work** (B1). Gate autosave and Ctrl+S on "a game is running", not on `screen === 'game'`. Show the ✓ SAVED indicator only when a write succeeded. Put Save / Load in Settings. Without this, nothing else persists for a real player.
2. **Give the great moments the stage.** One banner lane at top centre that toasts never cover. Hold or delay voice and tech toasts while a moment is showing. Do not fire a moment while a naming modal is open: queue it. Right now the best new writing in the game is hidden under "COSMIC WARNING".
3. **Turn SPECIES DIVERGES into a non-blocking toast** like the leader voices. Do not drop the speed for it, make Esc close it, and say whether the new lineage is a branch of *your* people.
