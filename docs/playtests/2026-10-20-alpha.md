# Alpha playtest — 2026-10-20

Build: `main` @ `289e2db` (v0.1.0). I played in headless Chromium at 1440×900 with software WebGL (SwiftShader).
Screenshots are in `/tmp/claude-0/-home-claude/d23aa3ae-9b61-5aff-aa71-d66f5decf8b3/scratchpad/alpha/`; below they are cited by file name only (e.g. `09-descend.png`).

**Caveat on timing.** The page ran at only **2–3 fps** in this environment (the dev overlay showed "2 fps"). Anything tied to frame count rather than wall-clock time (the cinematic, sim speed at 1000×) will feel much slower here than on a real GPU. Read my wall-time numbers as "relative", not absolute.
**Caveat on pacing.** My fresh game rolled **LIFE 20/20 and EVOLUTION 20/20** (`06-ready.png`), so the early game was about as fast as it can be.

Runs:
1. **Fresh game** from the title screen, through to an Interstellar civilisation. Home world "Veyra", species named "Lumari", religion "The Tidewatch".
2. **Dev run** with `?dev=1&planet=Orrin&cine=0&forge=0`. It started at Magma Ocean with no Forge, and I took it to microbial life.

Console: no page errors in either run. The only messages were a 404 on the title (the known mp3), WebGL "GPU stall due to ReadPixels" warnings, and a Canvas2D `willReadFrequently` warning.

---

## Verdict

Yes, I would keep playing, but mostly for the first twenty minutes.

The opening is excellent and feels authored, in a good way. The dice console, the Big Bang, the Forge with its Fate Cards and the whisperer's sealed golden card are all strong. The floating-disc diorama is gorgeous. The Species / Evolution screen is the clearest UI in the game.

Then the game hands me a civilisation and runs away with it. In about a minute and a half at 1000× my people went from "Early Civilisation" to "Interstellar". I had no meaningful decision on the way. The only thing that kept interrupting me was a modal leader dialogue at every era. My species was still listed as "A plant", "mindless", "Form II". My own evolution choices and my civilisation never felt connected.

The Chronicle's "Your hand is in this" is exactly the "I caused this" feeling the game promises. But I only got it once, from one Faith card, and the card's "What came of it" stayed "Nothing yet." 16 ages later.

## The first ten minutes

1. **Title** (`01-title.png`). The pixel logo "ETERNAL SYSTEMS", the eye and the slowly turning globe set a strong mood. There are three buttons. The bottom hint "SCROLL · PAN · CLICK STARS · [F] FOCUS · [ESC] PLANET VIEW" means nothing yet, because there are no stars on screen.
2. **Settings** (`02-settings.png`). It has a Gemini key field, "HAND-MADE SPRITES: ON" and an FPS cap. The buttons "ABORT" / "COMMIT" are a fun way to say Cancel / Save.
3. **Begin Genesis**, the seed console (`03-begin.png`). "Enter HOME_WORLD name to arm calibration." I typed "Veyra" and rolled four d20s one by one. This is delightful: each die has a subtitle telling me what it does ("War frequency · Conquest aggression"). Then the "DIVINE DOMINION ROLL" came up "09 WATCHFUL", and I never learned what that means.
4. **The Launch button was below the fold.** After the last roll, "▶ LAUNCH SIMULATION" sits at y≈853 in a panel that clips at y≈824 (`06-ready.png`). I had to scroll inside the panel to find it (`07-launch-scrolled.png`). On first sight it looks as if the game is waiting on something.
5. **Big Bang cinematic** (`08-cine-*.png`). There is a white flash, then plasma, the cosmic web ("Gravity draws them along the threads of a vast web.", `08-cine-24.png`), galaxies, and a dive to my sun. It is beautiful.
   - At 2 fps it lasted about **7 minutes** of wall time, against its designed ~44 s. The cinematic's clock seems to be frame-based.
   - The HUD (speed buttons, rails, Faith deck) was already in the DOM under it.
   - It ends on "A new star burns. Around it, Veyra is still fire." with "DESCEND TO VEYRA" (`08-cine-39.png`). Lovely handoff.
6. **The Forge** (`09-descend.png`). This is the best moment in the build. A molten disc, three Fate Cards ("The Green Possibility — Prepare the world for something that has not yet evolved. +11 forging") and "THE WORLD IS HARDENING… 0%".
   - Every pick visibly changes the world, and the line under the title responds ("The air thickens.", "Metal veins spread through the stone.").
   - "LET THE FIRE TURN (2)" rerolls the hand.
   - "LET FATE DECIDE" ends the Forge immediately at 80%. I expected one random card, not the end of the Forge. It is not obvious that this button ends it.
   - I could not tell what "+7 forging" means beyond "progress".
7. **"THE FORGING IS COMPLETE"** (`16-forge-complete2.png`). A clean summary: "Surface: A world-ocean, a few islands rising out of it", "Life potential: Exceptional". It feels like my choices produced this world.
8. **"THE LAST CRUST COOLS"** (`17-cool.png`, `18-cool-1000.png`, `20-god-open.png`). A countdown of about 1:23 with nothing to do. I pressed 1000× and the timer still counted down in real time ("1:23" → "1:12" in about 11 s, then "0:50"). This was the first time I felt bored.
9. **"A WHISPER"** (`22-after-cool.png`). "I am Bel the Vast. Take this. Break it when you are ready, and tell Keth Eternal nothing." A golden sealed card, "a gift". **This is the hook of the game.** I immediately wanted to know who Bel is and why Keth must not know. Breaking the seal shows "THE SPARK … life" (`23-seal-3.png`).
   - The card says "Life wakes in a sea hidden beneath the ice." My world is a warm ocean world with "Climate: Desert". There is no ice anywhere.
10. **"LIFE AWAKENS"** (`24-live-4.png`). A naming prompt: "Something lives on Veyra: one lineage, small and stubborn…". I named it **Lumari**. I was still at 1000×, and within seconds the toasts read "+3 DNA · Your species spread into beach" (`25-named.png`).
11. **About 10 seconds later** a leader I had never heard of, "DAX — king • Oshon • Theocracy", popped a modal (`26-seek.png`). Behind it the log read: "PRIMITIVE SPECIES", "INTELLIGENT SPECIES", "FIRST FIRE", "EARLY CIVILISATION: ANCIENT". Then "FAITH IS BORN" asked me to name a religion (`27-blessing.png`).
    - **Microbes to civilisation happened while I was reading one dialog.** I never saw a multicellular or complex stage on the disc. I had 135 DNA banked and had not spent a point.

## Confusing or unclear

- **My species and my civilisation feel like two different things.**
  - I named the species "Lumari", but the Species screen calls it "Micro Verdant Finling", then "Verdant Finling", tagged **"A plant"** with "Eats: light" (`29-species.png`, `33-living-after.png`).
  - The Your People panel calls them "LUMARI · Form II · Interstellar Civilisation" with the trait **"mindless"** (`61-people-late.png`).
  - Nations & Councils lists my people as "Verdant Finling — **wildlife** · 100% of its land" (`60-nations-late.png`).
  - So a mindless plant-fish built Dyson-era tech.
  - The Evolution tab still offers "Language", "Toolmaking" and "Bigger Brain" as future mutations after they reached space (`30-evolution-tab.png`).
- **The world had exactly one species** for the whole game ("1 living · 0 extinct · 1 in all", "PLANTS 1"). There was no wildlife, no rivals and no food web to look at.
- **DNA stopped mattering.** I had 135 DNA, spent 12 on three mutations, and held 123 DNA unchanged from the Ancient age to the Interstellar age. Nothing asked for it again.
- **"Seek a Sign"** opens three card choices "Keep one." (`40-sign.png`). It took me a moment to see that the kept card then lands in the Faith deck to be cast separately. The flow is good once learned.
  - "1 PRICE" is shown in red without saying what the price is until you hover. Then you see "Side effects: closed borders" (`41-price-hover.png`).
  - Hover tooltips are great.
- **"Faith is born"** asked me to name a religion while a leader dialog was still layered underneath it.
- **Divine Dominion "WATCHFUL"** and **Normal / Creative**: I never learned what Watchful does.
- **DP vs. DNA vs. TP:** three currencies show up in the top bar within a minute of each other (`28-paused-civ.png`). The TP one appears with a toast saying "Click TP on the star map to see your cards". In fact the TECHNOLOGY pile is right there on the planet view.
- **The council never changed.** Lorix Yleus / Fenen Ylan / Peleus Selon sat on Sholaia's council, with the same loyalty and influence, from the Ancient age to the Interstellar age 600K years later (`37-nations.png` vs. `60-nations-late.png`).
- **Pressures** in the People panel mostly said "Nothing presses hard on them now." Late game it was three identical lines, "The rivers of … run cloudy", one per nation (`61-people-late.png`). That is good wording, but repetitive.
- The text **"Keth Eternal: Someone has been speaking to you. I felt it."** appears in the chat after the whisper. That is great, and I wanted it to lead somewhere.

## Missing

- **A beat between first life and civilisation.** I never saw multicellular, complex or primitive life on the disc, because it all happened inside about 10 s at 1000×. I would expect the game to auto-slow (or pause) at each major stage, like it does for naming.
- **Auto-slow on big moments in general.** First nation, first religion, new era, and a supernova next door all scrolled past at 1000×.
- **A visible mark of my cast on the planet** (a landmark, a glow, a lichen patch), and a follow-up on what came of it.
- **A link between Evolution and civilisation.** Either the mutations I chose should shape the people (their traits, their sprite, their culture), or Language and Toolmaking should be required for civilisation.
- **Pressure that needs me.** Food sat at "1166% fed" and "2069% fed", and nothing went wrong until pollution late game. There was little reason to intervene.
- **Esc to close every overlay**, consistently.
- **A tooltip or legend for the Dominion roll** and for "+N forging".

## What works — keep this

- **The seed console with d20s** (`06-ready.png`): tactile, readable, and each die is explained.
- **The Big Bang cinematic** (`08-cine-24.png`): the cosmic-web shot and the captions are lovely, and "Descend to Veyra" is a great handoff.
- **The Forge** (`09-descend.png`, `16-forge-complete2.png`): the best loop in the game. Three cards, a world that visibly changes with each pick, flavour lines that answer my choices, and a summary that reads like a character sheet for my planet.
- **The whisperer and the sealed golden card** (`22-after-cool.png`, `23-seal-3.png`): a genuine narrative hook. Keep Bel the Vast and pay it off.
- **The floating-disc diorama** (`25-named.png`, `52-zoom-in.png`, `58-interstellar-disc.png`): waterfalls off the rim, floating islets, flags and growing cities. Zoom is smooth and stays readable up close (`53-zoom-more.png`).
- **The Species → Evolution tab** (`30-evolution-tab.png`, `31-queued.png`):
  - the stage columns ("MICROBIAL LIFE … INTELLIGENT SPECIES"), the "PICK ONE" groups, and "Gives / Costs them" with red drawbacks ("− Tied to the light: slow to move");
  - "Queue 3 changes, or one major change";
  - the silhouette reveal ("What? Micro Verdant Finling is evolving!" → "Congratulations! … evolved into Verdant Finling! (Form II)", `32-evolve-3.png`). It is a charming Pokémon nod.
- **Card hover tooltips** (`41-price-hover.png`, `43-lichen-hover.png`): Action / Works on / Target / Reach / Odds / Side effects is exactly the right info. The reach shown on the disc while dragging (`44-lichen-drag.png`) is great.
- **The Chronicle** (`47-chronicle.png`): "Every event remembers what caused it. Follow any of them back." and "✦ Your hand is in this. It began with THE LICHEN OF THE WASTES." This is the game's thesis made visible. More of it, please.
- **The TP card confirm** (`49-tp-played.png`): "Price: A sword looks for a use… Acts on: Free State of Sholaia · Leaves standing: a fortress". It is honest about consequences, and "The new wealth of Free State of Sholaia went to the few" really did show up in the Chronicle afterwards.
- **The Technology tree** (`59-tp-late.png`): the "Path of the Ages" timeline is clean.
- **The World Ledger** (`35-ledger.png`) and the sparklines in Your People (`61-people-late.png`): readable at a glance.
- **Rail and HUD**: GOD / PEOPLE / SPECIES / NATIONS / SYSTEM / CHRONICLE are clear. Double-clicking the planet opens Nations as promised (`55-dblclick.png`).

## Top 5 recommendations (by impact on feel)

1. **Slow the game down at each stage transition, and give the player something to do in it.** Auto-drop to 1× (or pause with a moment card) at first multicellular life, first intelligence, first nation and each new era. Right now microbes → space takes a couple of minutes, and the player watches the log scroll by.
2. **Make my species and my civilisation the same creature.**
   - Use the name I gave ("Lumari") everywhere.
   - Stop calling an interstellar people "A plant", "mindless" and "wildlife".
   - Make the Evolution path matter for civilisation: Language and Toolmaking should gate or flavour it.
   - Let DNA keep a purpose after civilisation.
3. **Replace the modal leader pop-ups with a non-blocking feed** (or batch them, or show them only when paused). They interrupt every era and they cover the Faith deck.
4. **Close the "I caused this" loop on the planet.**
   - Leave a visible mark where a Faith card lands.
   - Fill in the Chronicle's "What came of it", which still read "Nothing yet." after 16 ages.
   - Surface a toast like "Your Lichen let Luia settle the wastes" when consequences land.
5. **Fix the stale planet OVERVIEW panel and the hidden GOD chat in the planet view.** These are the two places a new player looks for "what is happening" and "how do I talk to the god", and both currently mislead. While there: make Esc close every overlay, keep LAUNCH above the fold, and let the speed buttons shorten the cooling countdown.
